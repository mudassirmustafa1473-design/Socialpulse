FAQ
Frequently Asked
Questions
How long before I see results? +
Most clients see measurable growth in engagement and reach within the first 30 days. Significant ROI from paid campaigns typically appears within 60–90 days of launch.
Do you create all the content? +
Yes — our in-house creative team handles everything from graphic design and copywriting to video editing. You only need to provide brand assets and approve content.
Which platforms do you specialize in? +
We work across all major platforms: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, X, Pinterest, and Snapchat. We'll recommend the best mix based on your target audience.
Can I keep my existing accounts? +
Absolutely. We work with your existing accounts and build on the foundation you've already established. No need to start fresh.
Is there a minimum contract length? +
We offer month-to-month plans, though we recommend at least 3 months for optimal results. Longer commitments come with discounted rates.
How do you measure success? +
We track KPIs aligned to your business goals — follower growth, engagement rate, website traffic from social, leads, and revenue attribution through UTM tracking.
